# Story and Plates information-architecture approval

**Architect Role:** assigned

## Approved artifact and scope

The production Ghost Signal visual language remains the approved Figma-equivalent artifact. The user explicitly approved moving the homepage’s personal narrative and timeline to a new **Story** page, moving the license-plate archive to a new **Plates** page, and retaining **The Signal** film as the homepage’s narrative evidence surface.

This approval covers route composition, route introductions, shared navigation destinations, responsive layout, and careful provenance language. It does not authorize a new visual identity or the presentation of supernatural, governmental, or interpersonal interpretations as independently verified facts.

## Invariants

- The same canonical theme.tools navbar, logo, Browse overlay, footer, and tokens appear on all three routes.
- Existing story and plate media are moved rather than discarded.
- Photographs and recordings are labeled as records of what was captured; interpretations remain attributed to the author.
- Homepage sensor, entropy, chat, and open-channel behavior remains intact.
