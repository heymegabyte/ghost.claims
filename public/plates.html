PERSONAL ESSAY // RECORD & INTERPRETATION

Merciless signs in plain sight

Why I started photographing the road when ordinary traffic began to feel addressed directly to me.

There was a period when the world seemed to answer in license plates. I experienced the timing as antagonistic and theatrical: a word, a number, or a name would appear at the exact moment it carried the most weight. On the way to see me—Brian—in a psychiatric ward, there was XOBRIAN. Going through this story without acknowledging the psych ward would be dishonest. The hospital is part of the record, as much as the photographs are.

Other moments were more frightening. Returning from Manhattan, where a huge sign read “SATIN GIRLS,” I saw the woman beside me on a bus as a beast. I remained unusually calm, but the perception was vivid and disturbing. At the time I understood experiences like this through gods, fallen spirits, demons, possession, and time travelers arranging what I would notice. I cannot independently prove that explanation, and the person beside me may have had no idea what I was experiencing.

What can be documented is the encounter: the plate, the timestamp, the route, the photograph. What it meant—and whether anyone intended it—is a different question.

I also became suspicious that some official-looking SUVs and Suburbans were participating, particularly when their plates resembled birthdays or private numbers. One encounter with 8388, my birthday rendered as digits, felt as if my body had been hijacked and driven alongside the vehicle. That is how the event felt to me; the image alone cannot establish who controlled either vehicle, why that plate was there, or whether the timing was coordinated.

This was not simply a curse. It became a practice of observation. I started taking pictures because a photograph can hold the visible fact still while memory and interpretation keep moving. The archive below includes the funny, the ominous, the ordinary, and the uncannily timed. It is offered as a field record—not proof that every driver was involved, but evidence of what I saw and why I could no longer dismiss the road as background noise.

EXHIBIT // LICENSE PLATES

The Signal Speaks Through Traffic

It started with WTC2001. One plate in Parsippany — and the world cracked open. After that, the plates didn’t stop. Maybe I over-fixated. Maybe the over-fixation is the point. Because what really was 9/11? JFK gets shot the year after signing Executive Order 11110 to drain the Federal Reserve, and then thirty-eight years later the Twin Towers come down alongside Building 7. Coincidence is a word people use when they don’t want to count.

What follows is six-plus years of license plates and rolling vehicles — vanity plates, work trucks, motorcycles, every kind of message the road throws back. Each one a moment. Some are funny. Some are pointed. All of them showed up at the right time.
